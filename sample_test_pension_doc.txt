# AC Nielsen (UK) Pension Plan – Scheme Information

Country: United Kingdom

Last Reviewed: November 2026

## 1. Scheme Overview

The AC Nielsen (UK) Pension Plan is an occupational pension arrangement associated with AC Nielsen's UK business.

The scheme contains two pension sections:

- Defined Benefit (DB) Section
- Defined Contribution (DC) Section

The scheme was closed to future benefit accrual with effect from 31 March 2016.

Closure to future benefit accrual means members no longer build up additional pension benefits under the scheme's previous arrangements. However, accrued pension benefits remain subject to the scheme rules.

## 2. Pension Scheme Structure

### 2.1 Defined Benefit (DB) Section

A Defined Benefit pension provides retirement benefits calculated according to the scheme's rules.

The benefits may depend on factors such as:
- Pensionable salary.
- Pensionable service.
- The applicable accrual rate.
- The scheme's retirement provisions.

The benefits already accrued by members remain subject to the scheme's governing documentation.

### 2.2 Defined Contribution (DC) Section

A Defined Contribution pension operates through an individual pension account or pot.

The value of the pension pot depends on:
- Contributions paid into the pension.
- Investment performance.
- Investment management charges.
- Applicable scheme expenses.

Unlike a Defined Benefit pension, the retirement value is not guaranteed and depends on investment performance and contributions.

## 3. Scheme Closure

The AC Nielsen (UK) Pension Plan closed to future benefit accrual on 31 March 2016.

This means:
- Members cannot continue building additional benefits through future service under the closed arrangements.
- Previously accrued pension benefits remain within the scheme, subject to its rules.
- Closure to future accrual does not automatically mean that the pension scheme has been wound up.
- Existing members may retain deferred pension benefits.

## 5. Transfers

Pension transfers are subject to the scheme rules and applicable UK pension legislation.

Members considering transferring their benefits should check:

- Whether their particular pension section permits transfers.
- Whether any transfer restrictions apply.
- Whether safeguarded benefits or guarantees could be lost.
- Whether independent regulated financial advice is required.

Do not assume that the scheme accepts incoming transfers or offers free transfers without checking its current documentation.

## 6. Trustee and Governance

The pension scheme operates under a trustee governance structure.

The trustees are responsible for overseeing the scheme in accordance with applicable pension legislation and the scheme's governing documentation.

For current trustee details, registered addresses and governance information, refer to the official scheme website and its latest published documents.

## 7. Important Pension Terminology

Defined Benefit (DB):
A pension arrangement where retirement benefits are calculated using a formula specified by the scheme rules.

Defined Contribution (DC):
A pension arrangement where contributions are invested to build an individual pension pot.

Accrued Benefits:
Pension benefits that a member has already earned through previous pensionable service or contributions.

Deferred Member:
A former contributing member who retains pension benefits in the scheme but is no longer actively building benefits.

Future Benefit Accrual:
The process through which a member earns additional pension benefits during pensionable service.

Pension Transfer:
The movement of pension benefits from one registered pension arrangement to another, subject to applicable rules.
